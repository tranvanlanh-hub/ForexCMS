# Change: Content Scale Operations Checkpoints 16-20

## Summary

Merge sessions 16 through 20 into one controlled content-scale operations change while preserving the required checkpoint order.

## Why

The CMS has foundations for content, templates, SEO, affiliate resolution, internal links, AI import, broker facts, analytics, and a small pilot seed. The next work needs a stricter AI brief contract, a 50-draft pilot, a repeatable content audit, a guarded large-batch pipeline, and operational notes for performance, sitemap, cache, and backup before any broader content scale-up.

## Scope

- Define the AI content brief and output schema for Article, Guide, BrokerReview, BestBrokerList, and CountryHub.
- Generate a deterministic 50-item pilot draft import data set across topic clusters and markets.
- Add an audit script for SEO metadata, duplicate slug/canonical paths, internal links, affiliate tokens, canonical paths, FAQ/schema readiness, and orphan drafts.
- Prepare a large-batch planning pipeline without creating 200-500 real articles until DB-backed AI import, SEO, internal link, and affiliate validation are green.
- Document sitemap chunking, cache strategy, query/index baseline, database/media backup checklist, and Cloudflare/VPS deploy notes.

## Out of Scope

- Do not production deploy.
- Do not use Cloudflare D1.
- Do not hard-code affiliate URLs in content.
- Do not change the public URL pattern.
- Do not create hundreds of real draft articles while DB-backed validation remains unavailable.
