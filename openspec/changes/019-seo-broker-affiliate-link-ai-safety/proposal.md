# Change: SEO, Broker, Affiliate, Internal Link, and AI Safety Checkpoints 26-30

## Summary

Merge sessions 26 through 30 into one sequential quality and safety change for the Forex CMS.

## Why

The CMS now has foundations for public content, SEO helpers, sourced broker facts, affiliate click tracking, internal link suggestions, and AI draft import. Before any larger content operation, editors need stronger audit dashboards, safer broker review/comparison surfaces, better affiliate click segmentation, stricter internal link rules, and batch-level AI import safety.

## Scope

- Checkpoint 26: add or upgrade SEO/AEO/GEO audit reporting for missing title/meta, multiple H1 risk, missing FAQ, missing schema, canonical mismatch, hreflang gaps, orphan content, and sitemap inclusion.
- Checkpoint 27: improve BrokerReview and BrokerComparison with structured broker facts, pros/cons, ratings placeholders only when sourced, fees/spread, regulation, deposit/withdrawal, platform data, and safe schema behavior.
- Checkpoint 28: upgrade affiliate click tracking dashboard by broker, campaign, market, content, and date while continuing to avoid unnecessary sensitive data storage.
- Checkpoint 29: upgrade internal link automation rules for topic cluster, market, language, priority pages, approval/semi-auto mode, link density, and cross-market/language safeguards.
- Checkpoint 30: add batch AI content safety with pre-import validation, dry-run mode, reject report, batch size limit, and draft-only import behavior.

## Out of Scope

- Do not production deploy.
- Do not change the established app architecture or public URL patterns.
- Do not hard-code affiliate destination links.
- Do not publish AI-generated content in bulk.
- Do not add personal tracking identifiers, cookies, fingerprints, IP storage, or user-agent storage for affiliate analytics.
