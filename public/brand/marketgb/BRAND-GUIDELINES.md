# MarketGB Online Brand Guidelines

This identity system was developed from the approved MarketGB logo and reference brand board supplied by the project owner on 13 September 2026.

## Brand foundation

- Purpose: make global financial markets easier to understand.
- Positioning: an independent financial publisher and comparison platform covering forex education, brokers and market knowledge.
- Brand promise: turn complex information into clear, useful insight.
- Primary tagline: **Insights for a brighter tomorrow.**
- Extended brand line: **Global perspective. Real insights. A brighter tomorrow.**
- Values: clarity, independence, progress and responsibility.

MarketGB is an editorial and comparison platform. It should not present itself as a broker, trading platform, bank, investment fund or signal-selling service.

## Logo system

- Preserve the original proportions and approved gradient.
- Do not rotate, stretch, outline, recolor or add effects to the logo.
- Minimum clear space equals the width of the symbol's right vertical stroke.
- Minimum horizontal-logo width is 160 px in digital applications.
- Below 160 px, use the standalone icon.
- `logo-primary.png` is for light surfaces.
- `logo-reverse.png` is for dark surfaces and dark photography.
- Monochrome variants are reserved for applications that allow only one color.

## Color system

- Primary Blue `#0B68FF`: primary actions, links and key data.
- Insight Teal `#00D4B8`: insight, progress and supporting highlights.
- Growth Lime `#7ED957`: controlled highlights only; never use it for small text on white.
- Deep Navy `#0A1F44`: headlines, dark surfaces and footer backgrounds.
- Light Gray `#E6ECF4`: secondary surfaces and dividers.
- Surface White `#F7F9FC`: default editorial background.
- Muted Text `#53657C`: supporting copy on light surfaces.

Use gradients selectively in the logo and selected brand moments. Most product and editorial interfaces should use solid colors.

## Typography

Use **Inter** across the brand and product system.

- Display and major headlines: 700-800 weight, compact tracking.
- Section headings: 700 weight.
- Body copy: 400-500 weight, 16-18 px, line height 1.55-1.70.
- UI labels and navigation: 600 weight.
- Tables and numerical data: 500-600 weight with clear alignment.
- Fallback stack: Arial, sans-serif.

## Voice and editorial principles

MarketGB sounds clear, evidence-led, calm and useful. Separate verified facts from editorial judgment. Explain costs, limitations and trading risk. Disclose commercial and affiliate relationships clearly.

Use:

- “Compare fees, platforms and account requirements.”
- “Trading involves risk. Review the conditions before deciding.”
- “Based on information verified on 13 September 2026.”

Avoid:

- Promises of profit or absolute safety.
- Fear of missing out and artificial urgency.
- Unsupported rankings or promotional superlatives.
- Language that presents MarketGB as a financial adviser.

## Core messaging

- Homepage headline: **Global insights for a brighter tomorrow.**
- Supporting line: **Independent broker comparisons and practical market education for better-informed decisions.**
- Primary calls to action: **Explore insights**, **Compare brokers**, **Learn the basics**.

- X cover: `x-cover-1500x500.png`.
- Icons: 16, 32, 48, 180 and 512 px.

Keep social headlines short and use one focal image. Do not crowd a frame with multiple messages, charts or competing calls to action.

## Source-file limitation

The approved logo was supplied as a high-resolution PNG. This kit is suitable for websites and online publishing. Obtain the original SVG, AI or EPS file from the logo designer before professional printing, large-format production or trademark filing. An automatically traced vector should not be treated as the master artwork.
