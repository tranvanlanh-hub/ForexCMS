# MarketGB Brand Guidelines

Status: **Official**  
Approved: 13 September 2026  
Primary domain: `MarketGB.com`  
Canonical language: English

## Brand foundation

MarketGB is an independent financial publisher and comparison platform covering
forex education, broker research, market knowledge, and country-specific content.
It is not a broker, trading platform, bank, investment fund, financial adviser,
or signal-selling service.

- Purpose: make global financial markets easier to understand.
- Promise: turn complex information into clear, useful insight.
- Tagline: **Insights for a brighter tomorrow.**
- Extended line: **Global perspective. Real insights. A brighter tomorrow.**
- Values: clarity, independence, progress, and responsibility.

## Messaging

- Homepage headline: **Global insights for a brighter tomorrow.**
- Supporting line: **Independent broker comparisons and practical market
  education for better-informed decisions.**
- Primary calls to action: **Explore insights**, **Compare brokers**, and
  **Learn the basics**.

All public brand and product copy uses English as its canonical source. Future
translations, if introduced, must derive from the approved English source and
must not redefine the brand position or claims.

## Logo system

Approved files are stored in `public/brand/marketgb/`.

- Use `logo-primary.png` on white and light surfaces.
- Use `logo-reverse.png` on dark surfaces and dark photography.
- Use `logo-mono-navy.png` or `logo-mono-white.png` where only one color is
  permitted.
- Use the standalone icon below a 160 px horizontal-logo width.
- Minimum clear space equals the width of the symbol's right vertical stroke.
- Preserve the logo's proportions and approved gradient.
- Do not rotate, stretch, outline, recolor, add effects, or place the logo over
  visually busy imagery.

## Typography

Use **Inter** across the brand and product system, with `Arial, sans-serif` as
the fallback stack.

- Display: 700-800 weight with compact tracking.
- Section headings: 700 weight.
- Body: 400-500 weight, 16-18 px, line height 1.55-1.70.
- UI and navigation: 600 weight.
- Tables and numerical data: 500-600 weight with clear alignment.

## Voice and editorial principles

MarketGB sounds clear, evidence-led, calm, and useful. Separate verified facts
from editorial judgment. Explain costs, limitations, and trading risk. Disclose
commercial and affiliate relationships clearly.

Preferred examples:

- “Compare fees, platforms and account requirements.”
- “Trading involves risk. Review the conditions before deciding.”
- “Based on information verified on 13 September 2026.”

Avoid promises of profit or absolute safety, artificial urgency, unsupported
rankings, promotional superlatives, and language that presents MarketGB as a
financial adviser.

## Source-file limitation

The approved logo was supplied as a high-resolution PNG. The current kit is
suitable for websites and online publishing. Obtain the original SVG, AI, or EPS
file from the logo designer before professional printing, large-format production,
or trademark filing. An automatically traced vector is not the master artwork.
